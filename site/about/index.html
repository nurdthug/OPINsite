About OPIN Global

We build the infrastructure behind real-world transactions.

A driver locked out of a car needs a locksmith and a clear price. A buyer approaching closing needs to know whose signature is missing. We build the systems that help people take that next step.

Our origins

Built from field experience.

Rowland Akinduro and Brian Mock co-founded OPIN Global. The idea behind OPIN Unlock came from a familiar moment on a locksmith call. Before a customer can decide what to do, they need to know who can help and what the job will cost.

That experience shaped how we design our products. The customer should see the price upfront, the professional should have the details before arriving, and both should know who is responsible for the next step.

Our portfolio

What we’ve built.

OPIN Unlock

“The in when you’re out.” Our iOS app brings vehicle, home, business and safe lockouts into one dispatch flow, with upfront pricing and secure in-app payment. Check current service availability.

Field operations

Our field work in the Coachella Valley and across Southern California includes 1Lucky Locksmith and Mobile Genius. Jobs range from residential lockouts to commercial exit devices, push bars and lock upgrades. Customers can compare quoted options before choosing the work.

Rapid website builds

An owner can walk us through their business on a call and see a working site take shape as we talk. For a local shop, that can mean getting its story, products, hours and directions online before the call ends.

AI transaction coordination

Our first AI transaction coordinator is built for real estate. It tracks documents, deadlines and the people responsible for them from contract to close, so the team can see what still needs attention.

MoonPod

MoonPod is our blockchain project. Its NFT presale platform gives collectors a way to participate in a digital release.

Digital art and media

Our creative work includes NurdThug Sacred Artifacts, a digital art collection released in 2021. It also spans co-founder Rowland Akinduro’s music as RowLow and his interviews as host of ChellaVision.

Our outlook

What comes next.

Dispatch across trades

Locksmithing is our starting point. We’re developing ways to match customers with nearby professionals by trade, availability and the work a job requires.

Tools for independent operators

A solo operator often handles the phone, the quote and the field visit. We’re building tools to carry job details from one step to the next, freeing up time for the work on site.

More services, more cities

As we explore new markets, we want customers everywhere to have the same clarity about pricing, payment and who will handle the job.

International outlook

Exploring Spain.

OPIN Global is exploring Spain as a future market, with an initial interest in Alicante and Valencia. We’re looking at how people find local tradespeople and where our experience in dispatch could help.

Why OPIN

Trust is the product.

Trust starts with a quote someone can understand. It grows when the right professional shows up, the payment is secure and the customer knows where to turn afterward.
