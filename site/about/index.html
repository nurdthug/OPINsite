About OPIN Global

Our work started with a locksmith call.

When someone calls from a parking lot with their keys locked in the car, they usually have two questions. Who can get here, and how much will it cost? We built OPIN Unlock around those questions.

Our background

The people behind OPIN.

Rowland Akinduro and Brian Mock founded OPIN Global. Rowland was born in Vienna, Austria. His work in locksmithing and mobile tech took him across Southern California’s Coachella Valley and to Oʻahu, Hawaiʻi.

On Oʻahu, Rowland worked in the field with 1Lucky Locksmith. Taking calls, quoting jobs and meeting customers at the door gave him a firsthand view of what dispatch software needs to get right.

New Hampshire is home to one of our co-founders. We’ve established business there through our web design work for local retailers.

Our portfolio

What we’ve built.

OPIN Unlock

Locked out of a car, home, business or safe? We built our iPhone app so you can request a locksmith, see the price and pay securely in one place. Check current service availability.

Field operations

Our field businesses include 1Lucky Locksmith and Mobile Genius. In the Coachella Valley, our work has ranged from getting a resident back inside to replacing the push bar on a shop door. On commercial jobs, we walk the owner through the options and quote each one.

Rapid website builds

We build websites directly with business owners. On a call, we can work through the photos, write the copy together and have a working page ready for the owner to review before we hang up.

AI transaction coordination

Our real estate transaction coordinator tracks paperwork and deadlines from contract to closing. It helps the team answer questions like “Whose signature are we waiting on?”

MoonPod

MoonPod is our blockchain project. We built its NFT presale platform for collectors.

Digital art and media

In 2021, we released the NurdThug Sacred Artifacts digital art collection. Rowland also records music as RowLow and hosts interviews on ChellaVision.

Our outlook

What comes next.

Dispatch across trades

We want to bring dispatch to more trades. The first question for each job is simple enough. Who’s nearby, has the right skills and can take the call?

Tools for independent operators

When you work for yourself, the phone still rings while you’re on a job. We’re building tools that keep the address, quote and request together so you can pick up where you left off.

International outlook

Spain Works

OPIN Global is exploring Spain through a new venture code-named Spain Works. Our initial focus is Alicante and Valencia, where we want to understand how residents find reliable tradespeople and how local operators book jobs.

If that work shows demand for a local dispatch service, we’ll consider outside funding to expand it. A Series A round is one possibility further down the line.

Why OPIN

A clear quote comes first.

Before anyone agrees to a job, they should know who’s coming, what the work covers and what it costs. That’s the standard we bring from the field. If you have a question about an existing request, contact OPIN support.
